My research lies at the intersection of corporate strategy, strategic human capital, and entrepreneurship. I examine how major corporate and institutional shocks — mergers and acquisitions, corporate bankruptcy, and changes in law and public policy — shape firm strategy, workforce composition, and individual careers. My research has been recognized with the AOM STR Division Distinguished Paper Award (2023, 2026) and an SMS Annual Conference Best Paper Honorable Mention (2023).

Originally from South Korea, I received my Ph.D. in Management (Strategy Area) from Purdue University, M.Sc. in Business and Technology Management from KAIST, and B.Econ. from Sungkyunkwan University. Prior to academia, I worked at Samsung Electronics and PwC.
